# 0181. Employees Earning More Than Their Managers
# Difficulty : Easy
# Tags       : database
# Solved on  : 2018-11-30
# LeetCode   : https://leetcode.com/problems/employees-earning-more-than-their-managers/

# Write your MySQL query statement below
select e.Name as Employee from Employee as e , Employee as f where e.ManagerId =f.Id and e.Salary >f.Salary ;
